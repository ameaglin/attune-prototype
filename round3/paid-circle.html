What is Attunement?

Attunement is the practice of noticing what's stirring in you — and learning to recognize God's voice within it. It is a Christian practice: discernment in partnership with the Holy Spirit, not a replacement for it, and not something outside that tradition.

↑
Why practice Attunement

Attunement fosters spiritual growth — toward a greater sense of connection, purpose, and peace.

What is a Circle?

A Circle is a small group that moves through a shared season of attunement together. You bring what you choose from your private practice to the gathering.
