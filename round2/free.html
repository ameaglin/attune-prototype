What is Attunement?

Attunement is the practice of noticing what's stirring in you — and learning to recognize God's voice within it. Attune is a tool to help facilitate discernment, in partnership with the Holy Spirit — not a replacement for it.

↑
What is Attune?

The app guides you through short reflection exercises, so you can notice patterns over time. Your reflections are personal to you — nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

↻
What is a Circle Community?

A Circle Community is a small group that moves through a shared season of attunement together. The app helps you prepare on your own; the Circle Community is where you gather.

🧭
How Does Attune Use AI?

AI is a focused pattern tool here, not a source of guidance. We use it to help roll up and name themes across your own exercises over time — a mirror for your own words, not an authority on them.

It only reads what you write in your own exercises. No content is saved to train a model or shared with anyone else — including your facilitator or group.
